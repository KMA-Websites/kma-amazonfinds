Privacy

What happens to your data?

The short version: I don't want your data. Here's what that actually means.

Privacy at a glance

GoodGear is designed to be a simple editorial product discovery website. I do not require user accounts, and I do not intentionally collect personal information simply because you visit the site.

Like almost every website, however, technical information may be processed when you access the site. This can include information such as your IP address, browser type, device information and the time of your request.

Hosting

GoodGear may be hosted using GitHub Pages. When you access the website, technical connection data may therefore be processed by the hosting provider in order to deliver the website and maintain the security and stability of its infrastructure.

The processing of technical access data is generally based on the legitimate interest in providing a secure and functioning website.

Search

The product search on GoodGear is intended to help you find content on the website. Search terms may be processed locally by the website's JavaScript and are not intentionally submitted to a separate analytics service.

Affiliate links

GoodGear may contain affiliate links, including links to Amazon. If you follow an affiliate link, the destination website may process information about your visit according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Important: Once you leave GoodGear and visit another website, that website's privacy policy applies to any data it processes. GoodGear does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

External links

GoodGear contains links to external websites, retailers and other third-party services. I am not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of those external websites.

I recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information or making a purchase.

Cookies and tracking

GoodGear does not intentionally use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or user profiling for its own purposes.

External websites linked from GoodGear may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Those technologies are controlled by the respective third-party provider.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data protection law, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including the right to access, rectification, deletion, restriction of processing and objection to certain processing activities.

If you believe your personal data is being processed by GoodGear and you have a question about that processing, you can contact the website operator using the contact information provided in the Impressum.

Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the website, services or applicable legal requirements change. The current version published on this page applies.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy or data protection relating to GoodGear, please contact the website operator using the contact details provided in the Impressum.
