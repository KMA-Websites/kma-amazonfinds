Affiliate Disclosure

Yes, links on GoodGear may be affiliate links. If you buy something through one of those links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Basically, you buy something you were going to buy anyway, and Amazon may throw a tiny amount of money in our general direction. As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases.

What that means for recommendations

Affiliate relationships do not mean that every product is automatically recommended or that products are ranked purely because they generate a commission. I aim to evaluate products based on usefulness, price-to-performance, features, quality, suitability and available customer feedback.

And Amazon?

Amazon does not run GoodGear, sponsor GoodGear, endorse GoodGear, or know what the hell we're doing. GoodGear is independently operated. Amazon simply happens to be one of the places where products may be available.

Transparency matters

If a product has not been properly researched yet, it won't be presented as such. We would rather have an empty product card than invent a review to fill it.
