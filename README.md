**“Okay... but which one should I actually buy in the end?”**

## ~ The Idea ~

<br>

The internet already has approximately **4.7 bigillion articles** telling you what to buy.
Some were probably written by a human.
Some were definitely written by Elon Musk’s intern, trying to sell you Tesla shares he printed in his kitchen.
But honestly, nothing competes with an autistic specimen,
37 tabs deep, hyperfixating on a €20 purchase like it’s Germany's GDP.

RAWR... Okay, let's not get weird now.

And so, **GoodGear exists to make the whole process slightly less painful.**

The mission: **Less guessing.**

`No pretending I personally tested something when I didn't.`  
`No mysterious “experts” who become rich overnight thanks to your purchase.`  
`No countdown timer telling you that your family will be personally assasinated if you don't BUY NOW.`

Just products, comparisons, useful information, and hopefully fewer bad purchases.
