About GoodGear

Good products.
Less searching.

The internet has approximately seventeen billion articles telling you what to buy. GoodGear exists because that is probably too many.

Why this exists

Someone has to actually compare the shit.

GoodGear is an independent product-discovery project focused on useful things worth considering. The basic idea is pretty simple: research products, compare the options, understand the trade-offs, and make the useful information easier to find.

There is already an absurd amount of product content online. Some of it is excellent. Some of it is clearly written because somebody needed another 1,500 words surrounding an Amazon link. GoodGear aims to sit somewhere in between: useful and transparent.
How I choose

Less marketing. More research.

I don't want to pretend I've personally tested every product on the internet. If something has been researched from specifications, customer feedback, manufacturer information and other available sources, that's what I'll say. If I haven't researched it yet, it stays a placeholder. No fake hands-on experience. No imaginary testing lab.
